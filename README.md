## 📈 About This Project

I originally built this trading journal as a personal project for fun, but I decided to share it with the trading community.

The goal is simple: to give fellow traders a practical tool they can use to document their trades, review their decisions, identify recurring patterns, and continuously improve their trading skills.

Whether you are a beginner building discipline or an experienced trader refining your strategy, I hope this project helps you become more structured, consistent, and intentional in your trading journey.

> Built for traders.
